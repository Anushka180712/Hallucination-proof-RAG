# Logistics and Cargo

Halcyon is resupplied by uncrewed Kestrel cargo capsules. A Kestrel capsule can carry up to 3,200 kilograms of supplies.

Resupply missions arrive roughly every 60 days. Each capsule docks at the Harbor port on the Atrium module.

Kestrel capsules are not reusable. After unloading, a capsule is packed with waste and returned experiments, then it re-enters the atmosphere. Lumen protein samples travel in a small heat-shielded return pod inside the capsule so they survive re-entry.

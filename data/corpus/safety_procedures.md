# Safety Procedures

Every crew member must complete 140 hours of emergency training before launch. Training covers fire response, depressurisation and medical emergencies.

In a depressurisation event, the crew gathers in the Haven module, which can be sealed off and has its own oxygen supply for 72 hours.

Spacewalks require two crew members outside and one crew member monitoring from inside. A single spacewalk may not last longer than 7 hours.

Smoking and open flames are never permitted anywhere on the station. The only exception to the open-flame rule is the sealed combustion chamber used by the Cascade program.

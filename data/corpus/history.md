# History of Halcyon

Planning for Halcyon began in 2034, when the Meridian Space Consortium was founded by four member agencies.

The first module, Keystone, was launched in 2038. Assembly took three years and required eleven launches in total.

In 2045 the Forge module was added to support combustion research. Forge was the seventh and final module, which brought the station to its current size.

The station is currently expected to remain in service until at least 2060.

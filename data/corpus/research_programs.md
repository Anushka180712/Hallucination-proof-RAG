# Research Programs

Halcyon hosts three main research programs. The Verdant program grows crops in microgravity and has harvested dwarf wheat, radishes and lettuce.

The Lumen program studies protein crystal growth for pharmaceutical research. Lumen experiments run for 30 days each and are returned to Earth on cargo capsules.

The Cascade program tests fire behaviour in microgravity. Cascade experiments are only allowed inside the sealed combustion chamber in the Forge module.

The station's chief scientist is Dr. Imani Okafor, who has led research on Halcyon since 2043.

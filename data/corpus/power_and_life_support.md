# Power and Life Support

Halcyon is powered by two solar arrays that generate a combined 240 kilowatts at peak sunlight. During orbital night the station runs on lithium-sulfur batteries with a usable capacity of 1,800 kilowatt-hours.

The oxygen generation system, called Breathe-2, splits water by electrolysis. Breathe-2 produces enough oxygen for 12 people, which gives a safety margin above the permanent crew size.

Water is recycled by the Aquifer loop, which recovers 96% of all water used on board, including humidity condensate and urine.

If both solar arrays fail, the station does not evacuate immediately. Emergency protocol requires the crew to shed non-essential loads and wait for ground control to approve an evacuation.

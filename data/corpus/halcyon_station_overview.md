# Halcyon Orbital Station Overview

Halcyon Orbital Station is a fictional research outpost used as the demo knowledge base for this project. Because every fact here is invented, a model cannot answer from memory and must rely on retrieval.

Halcyon was commissioned on 14 March 2041 by the Meridian Space Consortium. The station orbits at an altitude of 412 kilometres and completes one orbit every 92 minutes.

The station has a permanent crew of 9 people. Crew rotations happen every 180 days, and each rotation replaces at most four crew members at a time so that experienced staff are always aboard.

Halcyon is built from seven pressurised modules. The largest module is Atrium, which houses the galley, the exercise area and the main observation window.
